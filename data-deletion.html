User Data Deletion Instructions

If you would like to request deletion of your personal data associated with our application, please contact us using the email below.

How to request data deletion

Send an email to: support@izzcynovate.cloud

Please include the following information in your email:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address or phone number used with the service
  • A short message stating that you want your data deleted

We will process your request and delete applicable user data within a reasonable timeframe.
